Show the worked answer

Cycle the weekday case 1

In a calendar puzzle, today is Monday. Count forward 10 whole days, with tomorrow counted as one day later. Weekdays repeat every seven days, and no special dates matter. On which weekday does the counted interval end?

Show the worked answer

A. Thursday

10 days contain 1 complete weeks and 3 extra days. Full weeks preserve the weekday; advancing the remainder from Monday reaches Thursday. Counting today as day one would shift the result incorrectly.

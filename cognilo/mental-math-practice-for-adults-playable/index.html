Questions you will have

Does practising sums make me better at sums?

Yes, and that is nearly all the research supports. The 2016 Simons review in Psychological Science in the Public Interest found people tend to improve on the specific tasks they practise, with little evidence for broad transfer to other tasks. So: practise the sums you actually meet, which is what Cognilo's Real Numbers round is.

Will a brain-training app make me smarter or protect my memory?

No app on this page claims that, and Cognilo's listing says its ratings describe how you play Cognilo puzzles and are not a measure of intelligence or health. In 2016 the FTC fined Lumosity $2 million over claims like that and required competent and reliable scientific evidence for any future benefit claims.

How long is a session?

About six minutes for the Daily 5 at a typical pace, per the listing; days with the Market Run shopping scenario take a little longer, and after a few rounds the app shows your own estimate before you start.

Is it timed?

Cognilo is untimed by default (Precision); Sprint adds a timer and Zen is unrated. This browser drill is also untimed by default. An optional timer records time from your first typing in each sum until Check or Skip, excluding the explanations. Changing that option starts a fresh round. There is no deadline.

Is Cognilo free?

The free tier is complete, per the listing: the Daily 5, all six skills, ratings, the Benchmark and achievements. Pro is an optional monthly or annual subscription for the Performance Lab (Pressure Response, Interference), weekly and monthly reports, and up to 12 months of rating history.

Do I need an account?

No. Local-first, no ads, no tracking; history stays on the device and exports as JSON. Purchases and optional Game Center leaderboards need a connection; competition is off by default.

What we will not claim

  • No transfer promise.Practising sums improves sums. The 2016 Simons review found little evidence that brain-training gains transfer broadly; we are not going to tell you otherwise, and the app's listing says ratings are not a measure of intelligence or health.
  • There is a subscription.Cognilo Pro, monthly or annual, for the Performance Lab and reports. The free tier is complete and you can tap Continue Free on the paywall.
  • Ratings move slowly on purpose.Performance Ratings only move when several sessions agree, and the Benchmark uses held-out rounds. If you want a number that jumps after a good morning, this will frustrate you.
  • New app.The App Store listing shows the current version and device requirements.

How a day works

  1. Open it; the first puzzle starts.No sign-up, no questionnaire. Five rounds from five of six categories: memory, focus, logic, numbers, spatial, language.
  2. Get a result and a tip per round.Plain-language reason for why that puzzle was picked, a strategy tip, pace versus accuracy, and an unrated Review replay of anything that slipped.
  3. Come back tomorrow, or not.Rhythm counts days per week rather than a streak; Grace and Vacation cover busy weeks. Nothing punishes a missed day.

Who should not bother

  • Anyone wanting a cognitive assessment. See a clinician. This is a puzzle app and says so.
  • Children. It is a puzzle game for adults, per the listing; the sums are rent and restaurant bills, not homework.
  • People who want a streak to chase. Rhythm counts days per week instead. If the streak is your fuel, pick something else.
  • Android. iPhone and iPad only, iOS 18.

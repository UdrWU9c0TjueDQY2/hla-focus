Questions

Why don't iPhone Screen Time limits work for me?

Because the limit screen offers a way past it, and you know your own passcode. Apple's forums have threads going back years asking how to remove the extra-minute and ignore options. The limit is a reminder, not a wall, unless someone else holds the passcode.

What does a Pass actually do?

Per the listing: opening a protected app shows a native shield with two choices, Go Back or Use a Pass. A Pass is a length you pick (for example 5, 10 or 15 minutes) with an optional reason, and the relock is scheduled with iOS before the app opens, so protection resumes when the Pass ends even if FocusPass is closed.

Can I cheat it?

Yes, deliberately: Turn Off All Protection always works, by design. What you cannot do is cheat it by accident. The listing says Pro never sells a way around your own rules, and Task Access is capped at 3 minutes so it cannot become a loop.

Does it work with Apple's Screen Time or against it?

With it. FocusPass uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks and picker; Apple keeps your app selections opaque even to FocusPass. If a parent or organisation manages Screen Time, their settings come first.

What is free?

Native shields, Go Back and Focus Saves, timed Passes, automatic relocking, one Wallet, one Focus Zone, one Deep Focus session a day, 7-day insights, Momentum and missions. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription for more Wallets, Zones, Free Windows, Task Access, Adaptive Resistance, unlimited Deep Focus and full history.

Is there an account or tracking?

No account, no ads, no analytics SDKs, per the listing; rules and history stay on the device. Purchases are validated through Apple and RevenueCat with an anonymous ID.

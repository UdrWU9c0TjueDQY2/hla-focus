Questions

Why don't iPhone Screen Time limits work for me?

It depends on the settings and who manages them. Check whether a passcode protects changes and whether a parent or organisation manages the device. Apple documents passcode changes and recovery, so a passcode held by someone else is not a guarantee that the device owner can never change the controls.

What does a Pass actually do?

Per the listing: opening a protected app shows a native shield with two choices, Go Back or Use a Pass. A Pass is a length you pick (the walkthrough shows 5, 10 or 20 minutes) with an optional reason, and the relock is scheduled with iOS before the app opens, so protection resumes when the Pass ends even if FocusPass is closed.

Can I cheat it?

The listing says Turn Off All Protection remains available without a purchase. FocusPass supports deliberate, timed access; it does not promise tamper-proof controls or guarantee that settings and permissions can never change. Parent or organisation restrictions still take priority.

Does it work with Apple's Screen Time or against it?

With it. FocusPass uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks and picker; Apple keeps your app selections opaque even to FocusPass. If a parent or organisation manages Screen Time, their settings come first.

What is free?

Native shields, Go Back and Focus Saves, timed Passes, automatic relocking, one Wallet, one Focus Zone, one Deep Focus session a day, 7-day insights, Momentum and missions. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription for more Wallets, Zones, Free Windows, Task Access, Adaptive Resistance, unlimited Deep Focus and full history.

Is there an account or tracking?

No account, no ads, no analytics SDKs, per the listing; rules and history stay on the device. Purchases are validated through Apple and RevenueCat with an anonymous ID.

A ten-minute reply at 9:12

Fictional example

Sam wants to send one reply without spending the evening in a feed. At 9:12, Sam writes “answer the pickup question,” opens the app and sets a ten-minute limit. At 9:22, the visit is over. On the tracker, Sam records what actually happened: one reply sent, then four minutes spent scrolling.

The next evening, Sam opens the conversation directly. Comparing the two entries gives Sam a specific choice for tomorrow.

Questions people ask

Can I complete this without an app?

Yes. Each night has one observation or decision you can record on paper. The browser checkmarks are optional.

Why 9 pm?

It is a suggested starting time. If you want one hour before a 10:30 pm bedtime, use 9:30 pm. Choose a different time if evening work, caregiving or calls need to come first.

Does FocusPass block the whole phone?

You choose which apps, websites or categories to shield. Review the tools you need and test access before your evening window.

What happens when a Pass ends?

FocusPass relocks the protected app when the timed Pass expires. You can instead choose Go Back at the shield, which records a Focus Save.

What is included in FocusPass for free?

The free tier includes native shields, timed Passes, automatic relocking, one Wallet, one Focus Zone and seven-day insights. Pro is an optional subscription. It adds Task Access, more Zones and full history.

Where are my checklist marks saved?

The browser stores checkmarks locally when storage is available. Clearing site data removes them. The paper tracker and the iPhone app keep separate records.

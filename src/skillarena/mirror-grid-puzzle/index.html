How to solve a mirror grid puzzle

Start with one filled tile. Count its row from the top and its column from the left. Transform that location, then repeat for the other filled tiles. Empty locations matter too: an extra tile is still a mismatch.

Mirror left to right

Keep each row the same. Column 1 becomes 4, 2 becomes 3, 3 becomes 2, and 4 becomes 1. A tile at row 2, column 1 lands at row 2, column 4.

Mirror top to bottom

Keep each column the same. Row 1 becomes 4 and row 2 becomes 3. A tile at row 1, column 3 lands at row 4, column 3.

Rotate 90 degrees clockwise

For this four-by-four board, new row equals old column. New column equals 5 minus old row. A tile at row 1, column 2 lands at row 2, column 4. Unlike a reflection, four quarter-turns bring the whole pattern back.

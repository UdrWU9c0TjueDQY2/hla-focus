Questions people ask the test

What is a go/no-go task?

A signal appears; the player responds to one kind of signal and withholds a response to another. This original 24-trial browser activity illustrates that general instruction. It is not a validated version of a laboratory assessment and does not reproduce the SkillArena app's scoring.

How should I read this result?

Read it as a description of this run. Browser scheduling, screen refresh, input method, practice and distractions affect the counts and timing. There are no clinical thresholds, tiredness labels or population comparisons. Hiding the page interrupts the run and discards its result.

What does SkillArena actually claim?

Per its listing: it is a game, ratings describe performance in SkillArena's own games, and they are not an IQ score or a medical, psychological or clinical assessment.

Is SkillArena free?

The current US listing shows a free download and no listed in-app purchases. Offers can change; check the App Store before downloading.

How long is a session?

The Daily Arena is four games in about four minutes; Quick is about two and Extended about eight, per the listing. This browser activity is separate.

One invented runSuppose a run contains 18 blue signals and 6 red signals. The player taps 17 blue signals, misses one blue signal and taps one red signal. The other five red signals correctly receive no tap.

What followsThere are 22 correct responses out of 24 trials, approximately 92%. A displayed average of 420 ms would describe only the 17 recorded blue taps. Neither number establishes tiredness, everyday focus, a diagnosis or a benefit from the activity.

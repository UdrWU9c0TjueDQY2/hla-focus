LOOK FOR A FEATURE YOU CAN FOLLOW

A corner can be
your anchor.

Pick a distinctive part.

Look for a long arm, a short branch or an inside corner. Follow that feature as you imagine the shape turning.

Keep connections fixed.

Rotating changes which way the shape faces. It does not move a square to a different place within the shape.

Watch the mirror.

A reflected shape can look close. Its bend or branch may sit on the opposite side. Turning the target can help you compare.

What makes these six boards checkable?

Every shape contains six connected squares. The matching choice is made by turning the target in quarter-turns. The other choices are checked against all four orientations, so neither can become the target through rotation.

You can explore the rule without remembering a score. If a board looks tricky, turn the target, reveal the answer and look at what stayed connected.

RELIEF PRINTMAKING · PAPER EXERCISE

Which way will the linocut print?

Imagine the dark squares are the inked, raised parts of a block. Keep the top edge at the top in both views. Which impression would it leave on paper?

Check the proof

Proof B. The long upright moves from the left edge to the right. The short arm stays at the top and points the other way. Proof A is a quarter-turn of the block instead.

Try it on paper: draw the block on tracing paper, mark its top edge, then turn the sheet over from left to right while keeping the top edge up. Compare the back with the two proofs. This is a drawing exercise; no carving tools are needed.

The rules stay simple.

What is the difference between rotation and reflection?

Rotation turns a shape while keeping the same side facing you. Reflection makes a mirror image. In this game, only a rotated version counts as the twin.

Can I use the rotate button?

Yes. It turns only the target, one quarter-turn at a time. It is part of the exercise, not a penalty.

Is this a cognitive test?

No. It is a recreational shape puzzle. It produces no ability rating, comparison with other people or health result.

What happens after board six?

The next button becomes “Start again.” The same six boards return so you can examine the shapes again. This page does not generate an endless set or keep a history.

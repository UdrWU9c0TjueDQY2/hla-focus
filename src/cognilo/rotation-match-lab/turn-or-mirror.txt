TURN OR MIRROR?
Each pair has two 4 by 4 grids. X = filled, . = blank. Each row is separated by |. Decide whether the shape turns or reflects about the centre of the whole grid. Name the angle or mirror line.

Pair 1: XXXX | ..X. | ..X. | ....  becomes  XXXX | .X.. | .X.. | ....
Pair 2: .XXX | .XXX | .... | ....  becomes  .... | ..XX | ..XX | ..XX
Pair 3: XX.. | XX.. | X... | X...  becomes  X... | X... | XX.. | XX..
Pair 4: .... | .XX. | XX.. | XX..  becomes  .... | .X.. | .XXX | ..XX
Pair 5: .XX. | .XXX | .X.. | ....  becomes  .... | XXX. | XX.. | .X..
Pair 6: XXX. | XXX. | .... | ....  becomes  .... | .... | .XXX | .XXX

ANSWER KEY
1. Mirror left to right. Row 1, column 1 moves to row 1, column 4.
2. Turn: 90 degrees clockwise. Row 1, column 2 moves to row 2, column 4.
3. Mirror top to bottom. Row 1, column 1 moves to row 4, column 1.
4. Turn: 90 degrees counterclockwise. Row 2, column 2 moves to row 3, column 2.
5. Mirror across the top-left diagonal. Row 1, column 2 moves to row 2, column 1.
6. Turn: 180 degrees. Row 1, column 1 moves to row 4, column 4.

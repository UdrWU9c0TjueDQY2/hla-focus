A label reads LMNOPQ. Reverse the complete order of its symbols so the last becomes first, the next-to-last becomes second, and so on. Do not rotate the label or change any symbol. Which option is the exact reversal?

Show the worked answer

C. QPONML

The exact reversal is QPONML. Reading the original from its final position backward preserves every symbol while reversing every positional relationship. Moving only the first or last symbol would perform a rotation instead.

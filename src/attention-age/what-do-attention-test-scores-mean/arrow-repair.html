Arrow Repair

Change one arrow. Make the middle arrow and the majority follow their instructions.

Choose a tile to flip it. Choosing a different tile restores your previous choice. When you are ready, check both instructions. Take as long as you like.

Puzzle 1 of 6Exactly one change

Read both instructions

Middle arrow must point
At least three arrows must point

Choose one arrow to change.

The middle is tile 3. “At least three” means three, four or five arrows. More than one tile can sometimes solve a puzzle.

    Check the two instructions separately

    Read tile 3 first. Then count the arrows pointing in the requested majority direction. A repair can meet one instruction and miss the other. If the middle already points the right way, consider what changing an outside tile would do.

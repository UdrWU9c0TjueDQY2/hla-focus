A fictional one-week experiment records shorter screen sessions after a planning change, but other parts of the week also changed and no causal test was run. The owner wants a summary that reports the observation without claiming the plan alone caused it.

Show the worked answer

C. Shorter sessions were recorded after the change; cause is uncertain.

The record supports an observed sequence but not a uniquely established cause. Reporting that sequence with uncertainty preserves useful information, while claiming sole causation or denying the observation would go beyond or against the supplied evidence.

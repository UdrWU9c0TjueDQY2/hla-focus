Make it a habit with Time Debt

iPhone Screen Time vs an app blocker, in one paragraph

Start with App Limits in Settings if you want a scheduled limit. If you want an extra ten minutes today to reduce tomorrow’s allowance, Time Debt previews both days before you confirm. If you want a fixed session with different override rules, check the rules of the specific blocker you are considering.

Honest limits

  • Time Debt sits on top of Apple's Screen Time system and needs that permission; on your own iPhone you can withdraw it in Settings at any time. It is a voluntary commitment tool, not treatment, and the listing says so.
  • Emergency access removes every shield until tomorrow at no cost. That is a feature, and it is also the escape hatch; if you use it nightly, no method will hold and the honest next step is a different bedtime, not a different app.
  • It does not change Screen Time limits set in iOS Settings or by a Family Sharing organizer, and there is no remote monitoring; family approvals happen on the same device.
  • The core plan is free; Pro is a subscription. Price, trial eligibility and renewal terms are shown before purchase.

Who should pick something else

  • You have never set an App Limit: set one in Settings first. Two weeks of built-in Screen Time will tell you whether you are an Ignore-Limit person.
  • You want a wall with no door: Time Debt always leaves a door (borrow, or emergency access). Check a blocker’s override and emergency-access rules before enabling a session.
  • You want to manage a child's phone from your own: Time Debt's family tools work on a shared or handed-over iPhone, with approvals on that device, not remotely. Apple's Family Sharing Screen Time is built for remote management.

How borrowing works, in three steps

  1. Set a plan. Choose distracting apps, categories and websites with Apple's private picker and a realistic daily allowance; weekday and weekend limits can differ.
  2. Borrow with the full picture. When the allowance runs out, a shield covers the apps. Pick 5, 10, 15 or a custom amount within your cap. Today, tomorrow's normal allowance and tomorrow's adjusted allowance sit side by side. Nothing happens until you confirm.
  3. Repay tomorrow, once. Borrowed minutes come off tomorrow automatically, never twice, no interest. Tomorrow never drops below the floor you chose, and ending a borrowed session early returns the unused minutes.

An example evening, three ways

Screen Time: At 11:02 pm you reach an App Limit. Whether you can grant yourself more time depends on your settings.

Hard blocker: At 11:02 pm an active session blocks the chosen app. The exit and tomorrow’s schedule depend on the blocker you chose.

Time Debt: 11:02 pm, shield. Borrow 10? Today 1h → 1h 10m. Tomorrow 1h → 50m. Confirm. 11:12 pm, done, with a countdown on the Lock Screen. Wednesday starts ten minutes shorter, and History records it as borrowed and repaid, not as a failure.

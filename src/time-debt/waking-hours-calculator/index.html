How to calculate your screen-time percentage

The useful denominator is the time you are awake. A four-hour total is one-sixth of a full 24-hour day, but it is one-quarter of a 16-hour waking day. Neither fraction tells you whether that time was useful; the calculation simply makes the scale easier to understand.

Awake minutes = 1,440 − sleep minutes
Phone share = phone minutes ÷ awake minutes × 100

Count sleep across the same 24-hour period, including a nap if you want it included. The calculator accepts hours and minutes separately so that 4 hours 30 minutes stays 4 hours 30 minutes. Do not enter 4.30 as a decimal number of hours.

Start with a comparable number

Apple's Screen Time guide, checked November 4, 2026, explains how to view device-usage reports and select a device. Open Screen Time in Settings and find your activity summary. Menu labels can vary with your iOS version.

  1. Choose the iPhone you mean to measure. A combined report can include other devices.
  2. Choose a day, then enter sleep for the same 24-hour period.
  3. For a weekly average, use an average for both inputs. Avoid comparing a weekday phone average with a weekend sleep estimate.
  4. Check unusually high totals before drawing a conclusion. Simultaneous use across devices should not be added as if it were separate hours of your day.

This tool rejects phone time greater than waking time. That is a signal to check the inputs, the device filter or overlapping activity; it is not a judgment about you.

Give a smaller number a purpose

Pick a reduction you actually want to try, then choose what belongs in that space. Fifteen minutes might become a short reading break. Thirty minutes might become an uninterrupted meal. Necessary work, navigation, accessibility tools and meaningful conversations still count as screen time, so the biggest total is not automatically the least useful one.

The weekly comparison multiplies your chosen daily reduction by seven. It describes a scenario, not a prediction of time saved. If your target reduction exceeds your entered phone use, the calculator stops at zero phone minutes and uses only the reduction that is possible.

For different weekday and weekend allowances, use the weekly screen-time budget calculator. For the effect of borrowing extra minutes from tomorrow, try the borrow impact planner.

Questions about the result

Is 25% a good or bad score?

It is a fraction, not a score or diagnosis. The purpose of the time and what you would prefer to do matter. This page does not set a universal healthy limit.

Does the calculator measure my screen time?

No. You enter the numbers. It does not connect to Screen Time, request device permissions or collect your app history.

Does the weekly result mean I will gain free time?

No. It shows seven times the daily reduction you choose. What happens to that time depends on your actual behaviour and commitments.

Will Time Debt block every distraction permanently?

No. It works with Apple's Screen Time permissions for apps you select. Emergency access is available, and the device owner can withdraw permission in Settings.

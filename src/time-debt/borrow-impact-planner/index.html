The floor and cap do different jobs.

The floor preserves a minimum for tomorrow. The cap limits how much can be borrowed today. This example allows the smallest of your request, the cap and the minutes above tomorrow’s floor.

With 60 minutes tomorrow, a 20-minute floor and a 30-minute cap, requesting 25 leaves 35. Requesting 50 still only allows 30 in this model, leaving 30.

The preview assumes all granted minutes are used, and one repayment day with no earlier debt. In the actual app, check your active plan before borrowing; unused minutes can return.

What Pro adds

Reproduce the 15-minute example

In the borrowing calculator, set tomorrow’s allowance to 60, its floor to 20, today’s cap to 30 and the request to 15. Select “Show the tradeoff.” The result grants 15 extra minutes today and leaves 45 tomorrow. If today also started at 60 minutes, its total becomes 75. This example uses all 15 borrowed minutes, one repayment day and no earlier debt.
